# IFRS 18 Transition Brief

Nov 27, 2026 · saad muhammad

## Why it matters now

IFRS 18 *Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements* replaces IAS 1 for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, and it applies retrospectively. For a calendar-year company, 2026 is already the comparative year, so the data needed to restate it is being generated today. Recognition and measurement do not change, but the face of the income statement, key subtotals and investor-facing KPIs all do.

## Key changes

| Area | What changes | Practical impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income statement categories | Every income and expense item goes into operating, investing, financing, income taxes or discontinued operations. Operating is the residual category. | Equity-accounted results move to investing; interest on leases and pensions moves to financing. Banks, insurers and investment entities classify by their main business activities. |
| Required subtotals | Two new mandatory subtotals: operating profit, and profit before financing and income taxes. | Existing "EBIT" or "operating result" lines may no longer match and must be redefined. |
| Management-defined performance measures (MPMs) | Subtotals of income and expenses used in public communications (for example adjusted EBITDA) are disclosed in a single note. | Each MPM needs a reconciliation to the closest IFRS subtotal, with tax and non-controlling interest effects, and an explanation of why it is useful. MPMs fall inside the audit scope. |
| Aggregation and disaggregation | New principles set the roles of the primary statements and the notes. Use of "other" is restricted. | Entities presenting expenses by function must disclose five natures in a note: depreciation, amortisation, employee benefits, impairment losses and inventory write-downs. |
| Statement of cash flows (IAS 7 amendments) | Operating profit becomes the starting point for the indirect method. Options for interest and dividends are removed for most entities. | Interest and dividends paid go to financing; interest and dividends received go to investing. Goodwill is also presented as a separate balance sheet line. |

## Transition implications

- **Comparatives are restated.** The 2027 statements must present 2026 under IFRS 18, so ledgers, mappings and MPM data for 2026 must be captured on the new basis.
- **A bridge from IAS 1 is required.** In the first year, each line item in the comparative income statement is reconciled from its IAS 1 presentation to IFRS 18.
- **Interim reports go first.** Condensed interim statements in 2027 must already use the new categories and subtotals, so Q1 2027 is the real deadline.
- **IAS 8 disclosure starts earlier.** 2025 and 2026 annual reports should describe the expected impact of a standard issued but not yet effective.
- **Wider ripple effects.** Debt covenants, remuneration targets, analyst KPIs, systems and tax reporting that rely on today's subtotals need review.

## Transition roadmap

The Q1 2027 interim report is the first hard deadline (four-phase roadmap for a calendar-year company).

| Phase | Timing | Key activities | Gate at end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Diagnose | Dec 2026 | Map P&L lines to the new categories; inventory MPMs; size covenant impact | Impact agreed with auditors |
| 2. Design | Dec 2026 to Jan 2027 | Update chart of accounts mapping; draft the MPM note; mock 2026 statements | Audit committee approves mock statements |
| 3. Restate | Jan to Apr 2027 | Restate 2026 comparatives; build the IAS 1 bridge; issue Q1 interim | **Q1 2027 interim issued on the new basis** |
| 4. Embed | Q2 to Q4 2027 | Update systems, KPIs and covenants; investor messaging; first annual report | First IFRS 18 annual statements |

With 2026 nearly over, the gap between today and the first IFRS 18 interim report is about five months. Start with the line-by-line mapping, since every later step depends on it.
